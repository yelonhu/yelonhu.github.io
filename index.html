Hi 👋! I am Jinyuan Hu (胡锦源), an undergraduate student studying Computer Sciences at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. I transferred from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

I am also a research intern at CUHK MMLab, working with Prof. Xiangyu Yue.

I am interested in learning and adaptation through interaction and feedback. Moving forward, I aim to focus on robotics. This includes adapting robot policies to contact-rich manipulation with limited new experience.

I build and run an SO-101 arm at home for closed-loop experiments. Lab notes.
