nsfz.svg
Locally traced from the crest region of nsfz.png using VTracer 0.6.15.
The campus-specific wordmark was excluded (source crop: 0, 7, 59, 79).
Tracing input was composited on white, upsampled 4x and quantized to five
colors without dithering. The result contains SVG paths, not an embedded
bitmap. This is an approximate trace of a low-resolution source, not an
official vector master; small lettering retains the source's limitations.
Tool: https://github.com/visioncortex/vtracer
