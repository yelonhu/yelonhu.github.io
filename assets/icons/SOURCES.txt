Y favicon
Original drawing for this site (no third-party artwork).

favicon.svg: the letter Y drawn as a machined linkage bracket. It is one
filleted plate whose arms taper toward the ends, in anodized blue (#1772D0),
with a pivot bore at the joint and a mounting hole at each end. It reads as a
Y at tab size and as a robot part up close, and the blue stays visible on both
light and dark browser tabs.
../../favicon.ico (16/32/48 px) and apple-touch-icon.png (mark centered on
white, 180 px) are rasterized from the same SVG.
